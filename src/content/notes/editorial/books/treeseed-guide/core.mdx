The TreeSeed Guide follows a community project from shared purpose through platform setup, identity and teams, content, agent work, cooperative governance, and participation in the market.

Every page identifies its primary and secondary audiences from community members, developers, operators, and AI agents. Audience needs are lenses, not duplicate books. Introduce why a capability matters, identify its state and authority, explain the useful model or procedure, make failure and recovery visible when relevant, and connect the reader to prerequisite and next-step pages.

Guarantees are normative promises. Guide prose explains them without silently widening them. Detailed evidence belongs in linked editorial notes; published pages remain concise and source-grounded. Technical and audience review are independent, and a human Book Owner approves the exact chapter revision before publication.
