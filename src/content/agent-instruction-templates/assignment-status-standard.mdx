  Report the current phase, useful completed outcome, next bounded action, remaining time, blockers, and changed artifact identities. Use an append-only sequence and exact previous-status reference.   ## Current phase
  ## Useful outcome
  ## Next action
  ## Time and blockers
  ## Artifact and evidence refs

appliesToProfiles: [planning, chat, estimating, acting, reviewing, reporting]
