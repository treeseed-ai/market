The TreeSeed market is the integrated reference tenant for the platform. It proves public marketing and Knowledge Hub surfaces, authenticated operational market work, authenticated product surfaces, service readiness, agent operating loop, ecommerce, and Commons governance as one architecture rather than as separate mini-apps.

This book exists as the stable route and reference target for market architecture links across templates, objectives, and visual audit scenes. Its sections are assembled from the canonical architecture documents instead of duplicating their implementation details.
