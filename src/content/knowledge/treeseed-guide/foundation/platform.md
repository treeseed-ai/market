# Platform

People authenticate through accounts and participate through teams. Teams own or steward projects, and projects organize templates, knowledge, services, agents, and work. Capacity providers supply bounded execution resources; workdays coordinate useful activity; guarantees make expected behavior inspectable. Proposals and decisions govern change, while the market supports accountable exchange around products and services.

Use the platform map to enter [Deployment](../deployment), [Security](../security), [Content](../content), [Work](../work), [Governance](../governance), or [Market](../market). Important starting points include [Accounts](../security/accounts), [Teams](../security/teams), [Projects / Templates](../content/projects-templates), [Agents](../work/agents), [Capacity Providers](../work/capacity-providers), [Cooperative Decisions](../governance/cooperative-decisions), and [Ecommerce](../market/ecommerce).

The architecture book expands the governance and commerce boundaries in [Commons governance](/t/treeseed/books/platform-architecture-development/commons-governance) and [Ecommerce platform](/t/treeseed/books/platform-architecture-development/ecommerce).
