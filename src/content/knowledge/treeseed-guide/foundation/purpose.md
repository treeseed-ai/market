# Purpose

TreeSeed is a knowledge, collaboration, and execution platform that connects durable content, accountable work, shared governance, and exchange. This Guide gives users, team owners, project managers, operators, contributors, and machine agents a common map of the platform's expected capabilities.

Guarantee pages describe normative product promises that should be supported by repeatable evidence. Foundation pages and the related architecture book provide explanatory context: they show how those promises fit together without replacing the guarantees as the source of expected behavior.

Humans can follow the table of contents and page links. Machines can traverse the same structure through stable IDs, `parentId`, `relatedKnowledgeIds`, guarantee associations, and parsed Markdown frontmatter.
