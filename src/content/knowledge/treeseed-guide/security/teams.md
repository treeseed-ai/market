# Teams

## Basic team journey

1. Create a team or accept an invitation.
2. Switch the active team and review its overview.
3. Configure profile, visibility, and settings.
4. Invite members and manage outstanding invitations.
5. Understand roles, change roles, and remove members.
6. Protect the last owner and review membership history.
7. Archive, restore, or delete the team safely.
8. Use team-scoped projects, services, vaults, capacity, work, and governance.
