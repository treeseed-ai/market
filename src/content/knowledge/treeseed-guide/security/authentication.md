# Authentication

## Basic authentication journey

1. Register and verify an email address.
2. Sign in and sign out safely.
3. Recover a forgotten password.
4. Manage sessions and reauthenticate for sensitive actions.
5. Configure, challenge, and recover multi-factor authentication.
6. Link supported identity providers and preserve safe return paths.
7. Continue into account and team setup.
