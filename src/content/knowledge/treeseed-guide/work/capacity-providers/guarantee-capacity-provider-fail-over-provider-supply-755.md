# Fail Over Provider Supply Safely

Team policy may rank fresh, approved, capability-compatible provider sessions. Automatic failover is allowed only when prior work has no usage, successful mode run, TreeDX mutation, fallback output, or uncertain settlement; every earlier attempt remains immutable evidence.

This page documents `guarantee.capacity.provider.fail-over-provider-supply.755`. Its current guarantee status is **planned** pending multi-provider acceptance and live failover scenes.
